Covered RV, Boat & Auto Facilities

Covered 14′ bays, enclosed 12′×30′ bays, free dump station, water & air.

+

Texas sun and weather are hard on RVs, boats, autos and motorcycles. UV damages tires, seals, rubber roofs, caulking and paint. We offer covered and open storage for large and small RVs, plus enclosed storage for autos, motorcycles and boats.

Our 14-foot-wide bays give you room to load storage bins, open slide-outs and do the manufacturer's recommended lubrication while your rig is stored. Enclosed auto and boat bays are 12′ × 30′ with overhead doors. Some bays have 110V or 220V power, and covered bays without electricity are also available.

  • 12′×30′ enclosed auto & boat bays
  • Covered 14′×31′ & 14′×45′ bays
  • Free dump station for customers
  • Free water & air for customers
  • Personal gate code or optional remote
  • Fenced, lighted & camera monitored
  • All bays easily defined & divided
  • Short & long term rental

Storage Size Guide

5×10 & 6×10

About the size of a large walk-in closet. A studio or 1-bedroom: mattress set, dresser, boxes, a bike.

8×10 to 10×10

10×12 to 12×12

Frequently Asked Questions

Fees, hours, payments, RV amenities and more.

+
What are your office hours?

Our office is open Monday–Friday 9:30am–5:00pm and Saturday 9:00am–12:00pm. Renters have gate access seven days a week with a personal access code.

Are there any move-in fees?

A one-time $20 non-refundable admin fee applies to all units. Every unit must be secured with a round disc lock, which is available in our office.

What temperature are climate controlled units kept at?

Climate controlled units are kept between about 65 and 85 degrees year-round.

Do you offer RV and boat storage in Tyler?

Yes. We have covered 14′×31′ and 14′×45′ bays, enclosed 12′×30′ auto and boat bays with overhead doors, and outside parking. Customers get a free dump station plus free water and air. Some bays have 110V or 220V power.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, checks, money orders and cash. You can also pay your bill online any time.

Can I reserve a unit online?

Yes. Reserve online with no payment due until move-in, or rent online and finish your paperwork in our office. Reservations are held for 24 hours.

Do you sell boxes and moving supplies?

Yes. Boxes and moving supplies are available in our office, along with the round disc locks required for all units.

Is storage insurance available?

Yes. Low-cost insurance is available. Ask in the office when you rent.
